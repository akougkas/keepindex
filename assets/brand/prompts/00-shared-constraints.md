# Shared generation constraints · v1.0.0

Prepend this block to every KeepIndex visual-generation prompt.

```text
You are creating supporting brand imagery for KeepIndex, a private search on your computer with your choice of AI product. The canonical promise is “Search your world. Keep it yours.” The product is not a chatbot and must not look like generic artificial-intelligence marketing.

Use the attached KeepIndex vector mark only as an immutable composition reference. Do not redraw it, reinterpret it, add detail to it, place it as illegible decoration, or generate any lettering. The production designer will overlay the real SVG and real typography after generation.

Core visual grammar: four clearly differentiated ivory evidence paths represent web, a private vault, local documents, and opt-in browser history. They converge through a compact, protected woven K/I lattice. Exactly one green path emerges as the grounded, cited result. The feeling is rigorous, quiet, human, and owned by the user.

Palette: near-black dark ink #071117 as the dominant field; warm ivory #F2EFE6 for source material; evidence green #62D66B as the only saturated accent; restrained stone #A7A59B for secondary detail. No purple, blue-purple, cyan-magenta, rainbow, iridescent, or vaporwave gradient.

Material and lighting: editorial product photography or mathematically precise graphic construction, controlled low-key illumination, tactile fibers/paper/etched metal when requested, clean shadow detail, very fine grain, no glossy science-fiction interface.

Hard exclusions: no magnifying glass, padlock, keyhole, shield as the main symbol, castle, fortress, Greek column, sparkle, starburst, magic wand, robot, android, face, brain, eyeball, chatbot bubble, cloud-computing icon, generic AI glyph, glowing neural network, circuit-board globe, binary rain, stock server racks, crypto imagery, surveillance camera, or corporate handshake.

Text exclusions: render no words, letters, numbers, URLs, citations, logos, labels, watermarks, signatures, interface text, or pseudo-text. Do not invent a KeepIndex logo. Leave the specified text-safe region visually calm.

Privacy exclusions: no real personal data, browser history, email, credentials, API keys, cookies, private filenames, identifiable home interiors, or legible screen content.

Output must be a polished single composition, not a contact sheet, mood board, logo grid, brand-guideline page, or mockup collage, unless the task explicitly requests variants.
```
